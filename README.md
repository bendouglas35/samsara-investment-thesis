# Samsara 2035 Investment Thesis

**Independent investment research by Ben Douglas · 15 September 2026**

Can Samsara become an operational decision layer for the physical economy?

[Read the investment thesis](Samsara_2035_Investment_Thesis.pdf)

## Investment question

The research examines whether Samsara can expand from telematics into the workflows through which physical businesses detect problems, take action and measure results. It connects company filings and investor materials with an operator-informed view of fleet and workshop processes.

## Approach

- Company and industry research, including enterprise adoption and product expansion.
- A “detect, act, prove” framework for testing customer value and switching costs.
- Bear, base and bull scenarios, with explicit treatment of dilution and terminal valuation.
- A 20,000-run Monte Carlo valuation and conditions that would challenge the thesis.
- An illustrative UK operator case study, with assumptions distinguished from observed company data.

## Central judgement

The report is positive on the potential business but valuation-sensitive. Its conclusion is a watchlist position: monitor whether emerging products become material, enterprise expansion remains broad-based, and stock-based compensation translates into acceptable per-share economics.

## Contents

The 12-page PDF includes the executive summary, strategic and competitive analysis, operator case study, financial quality, scenario valuation, Monte Carlo analysis, risks and references.
